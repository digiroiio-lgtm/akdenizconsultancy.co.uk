Warranty and Guarantee Information

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd provides healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support for patients considering dental treatment overseas.

The UK company does not provide clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning or dental treatment and does not independently issue clinical treatment warranties or guarantees.

Any clinical warranty or guarantee is provided by the relevant independent overseas treating clinic and is subject to that clinic's own warranty terms, conditions, exclusions, eligibility requirements and clinical assessment.

Examples of warranties that may apply

Examples of warranties that may apply through the overseas clinics we work with include:

2-Year Dental Crown Warranty

Issued by the relevant treating overseas clinic and subject to that clinic's applicable warranty terms and conditions.

5-Year Dental Implant Warranty

Applicable where supported by the relevant implant manufacturer's warranty and subject to the manufacturer's warranty terms, together with any applicable conditions of the treating overseas clinic.

Manufacturer warranties apply only to the products, components and circumstances expressly covered by the relevant manufacturer and do not automatically constitute a guarantee of clinical treatment outcome.

Our role in warranty coordination

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd may assist patients with communication and administrative coordination in relation to a warranty claim. Responsibility for assessing eligibility, clinical circumstances and any remedy remains with the relevant treating clinic and/or manufacturer, as applicable.

Further information

Representative clinic warranty terms and, where available, manufacturer warranty documentation can be provided for due-diligence purposes. Please contact us at support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk.
