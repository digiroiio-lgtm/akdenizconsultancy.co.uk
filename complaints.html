Company Complaints Procedure

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd

Version: 1.0
Responsible officer: Complaints Manager

1. Our commitment

We aim to provide a clear, reliable and respectful patient coordination service. We welcome feedback and handle complaints fairly, promptly and without charge. Making a complaint will not result in unfair treatment or affect your statutory rights.

2. What this procedure covers

This procedure covers our healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support, including communications, appointment arrangements, information provided by us, and any fees charged for our services.

We do not provide clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning or dental treatment. These services are provided by the overseas clinic or clinician identified in your treatment documentation.

Where a complaint concerns both our service and clinical care, we will investigate our own involvement and, with appropriate authority, help you raise the clinical concerns with the treating provider. We will not reject a complaint about our own conduct merely because a third party is also involved.

3. How to complain

Please contact support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk or write to the Complaints Manager at our registered office listed below.

Please provide your name, contact details, a description of the issue, relevant dates and the outcome you are seeking. Supporting documents may help, but you do not need to provide every document before raising a complaint.

You may appoint a representative. We may request appropriate authority before discussing personal information with them. Please tell us if you need assistance or an accessible format.

4. How we handle complaints

We will:

  • Acknowledge your complaint within five working days.
  • Identify the person responsible for reviewing it.
  • Review relevant records and ask for any necessary clarification.
  • Aim to provide a written final response within 20 working days.

If we need longer, we will explain why, tell you what remains outstanding and provide a revised response date. Our response will explain our findings, any proposed remedy and available next steps.

Where appropriate, remedies may include an explanation, apology, correction, repeat performance of our service, or a refund or price reduction.

5. Further review

If you remain dissatisfied, reply explaining the points you wish us to reconsider. A director or another suitably senior person, who was not directly involved where practicable, will review the matter. We aim to respond within ten working days.

Our final response will identify any applicable independent dispute-resolution route and explain whether we are required or willing to participate. You do not have to complete our internal review before exercising any independent legal right.

6. Clinical concerns and urgent care

The treating provider is responsible for investigating clinical complaints and explaining its clinical review, aftercare and any applicable warranty process. We can assist with communication but cannot determine clinical liability or promise a particular clinical remedy.

This complaints service is not an emergency medical service. If you need urgent care, contact an appropriate local healthcare service immediately.

7. Finance-related complaints

If your complaint concerns a lender, credit broker or finance introduction, we will identify the responsible firm and explain the appropriate complaints route. Where appropriate, we will promptly forward the relevant complaint information and tell you that we have done so.

We remain responsible for investigating any complaint about our own conduct. Any applicable regulatory complaint-handling requirements take precedence over our ordinary service targets.

Where the complaint falls within the jurisdiction of the Financial Ombudsman Service, the responsible firm's response will explain your referral rights and applicable deadlines.

8. Personal information and records

We use complaint information to investigate, respond and improve our services. Information is shared only where necessary and lawful and retained in accordance with our documented retention arrangements and applicable legal requirements.

You may also raise concerns about our handling of personal information with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

We review this procedure annually and after significant changes to our services.
