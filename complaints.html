Complaints Procedure

We aim to handle concerns fairly, promptly and transparently.

  1. Email your complaint to support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk with your name, contact details and a clear description of the issue.
  2. We will acknowledge receipt and review matters relating to our consultancy, coordination or administrative support.
  3. We will provide a response or an update if more time is reasonably required.

Complaints about clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment or clinical outcomes must be addressed to the independent overseas clinic or treating clinician. We can assist with communication but cannot determine clinical complaints on their behalf.
