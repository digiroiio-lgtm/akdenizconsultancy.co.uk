What we do

Clear support at every stage

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd provides healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support for UK patients considering dental treatment overseas. All clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning and dental treatment are carried out solely by independent overseas dental clinics and clinicians.

Patient enquiries

Clear, practical support when you are considering treatment overseas.

Appointment coordination

Administrative help arranging consultations and appointments with independent providers.

Provider communication

Coordination between you and the overseas dental clinic throughout your journey.

Travel support

Help organising treatment journey details and related travel administration.

Patient journey

A coordinated, transparent process

  1. Enquiry
  2. Initial coordination
  3. Overseas clinic review
  4. Appointment coordination
  5. Travel planning
  6. Treatment with overseas provider
  7. Aftercare coordination

Finance

Administrative support where applicable

Finance options may be available subject to eligibility, status and the terms of the relevant finance provider. Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd may assist patients with the administrative process where applicable. Finance is not guaranteed.

Important

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd does not provide dental treatment, clinical diagnosis or clinical treatment planning. All clinical services are provided independently by overseas dental clinics and qualified clinicians.
