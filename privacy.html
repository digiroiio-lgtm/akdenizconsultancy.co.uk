Privacy Policy

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd provides healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support for UK patients considering dental treatment overseas. All clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning and dental treatment are carried out solely by independent overseas dental clinics and clinicians.

We use personal information to respond to enquiries and provide patient coordination and administrative support. Where necessary and with an appropriate lawful basis, information may be shared with the independent overseas provider you are considering and with relevant service providers.

We take reasonable measures to keep information secure and retain it only as long as necessary for the purpose collected and applicable legal obligations.

You may ask about access, correction, deletion or other data protection rights by emailing support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.
