This policy explains how Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd uses personal information when providing healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support to UK patients considering dental treatment overseas.

1. Who is the controller?

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd, company number 17408893, is the controller for personal information used for our own consultancy, coordination and administrative services. Our registered office is 14 Grove Green Road, London, E11 4EJ, England. Contact us at support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk.

An overseas clinic or clinician may be a separate controller for information used to assess suitability, plan or provide clinical treatment. Its own privacy information should explain how it uses that data.

2. Personal information we may collect

  • Contact and enquiry data: name, email address, telephone number, preferred contact method and the content of your enquiry.
  • Identification and eligibility data: date of birth, address, identity or travel document details where reasonably required for coordination, verification or travel-related administration.
  • Treatment-related and health information: dental concerns, medical history relevant to the enquiry, proposed or previous treatment and information supplied by you or a provider.
  • Photos and documents: dental photographs, X-rays or scans, treatment plans, quotations, consent or aftercare documents and correspondence, where you choose to provide them or ask us to coordinate them.
  • Journey and administration data: appointment preferences, travel details, provider communications, complaints, service records and payment records relating to our own services.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser information, security logs and cookie preference data.

Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry. If you provide information about another person, you should have authority to do so.

3. How we obtain information

We may receive information directly from you, from a representative authorised by you, from an independent clinic or clinician, or from a service provider involved in your requested coordination. We may also receive limited technical information when you use this website.

4. Why we use information and our lawful bases

  • To respond to enquiries and take steps at your request before entering a service agreement: contract or steps before a contract.
  • To provide agreed coordination and administrative services: contract.
  • To operate, secure and improve our service, maintain records and manage complaints: our legitimate interests, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • To meet accounting, regulatory, safeguarding or other legal requirements: legal obligation.
  • For optional communications or uses where another basis is not appropriate: consent, which you may withdraw.

Health information is special category personal data. Where we need it for coordination, we will normally rely on your explicit consent under Article 9 UK GDPR. In limited circumstances another condition may apply, such as protecting vital interests where you cannot consent, or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. We do not use health information for unrelated marketing.

You are not required to provide personal information, but without information reasonably needed for a requested service we may be unable to coordinate your enquiry or communicate with a provider.

5. Who we may share information with

Where necessary and lawful, we may share relevant information with the independent overseas clinic or clinician you are considering, providers supporting appointments or travel administration, IT and secure communications suppliers, professional advisers, insurers, payment or finance providers involved at your request, and public authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

We share only what is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose. Independent providers are responsible for their own use of personal information.

6. International transfers

An overseas provider may be located outside the UK, including in a country that may not have UK adequacy regulations. Before making a restricted transfer, we will consider the destination, recipient and appropriate legal mechanism. Depending on the circumstances, this may include UK-approved contractual safeguards or a specific legal exception. Where explicit consent is the relevant transfer mechanism, we will explain the possible risks before asking for it.

You may ask us for information about the safeguards relevant to a transfer concerning you.

7. Service providers and security

Suppliers processing information for us must act on our instructions, protect the information and use it only for agreed purposes. We use reasonable organisational and technical measures designed to prevent unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. How long we keep information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected. We consider the duration of the enquiry or service relationship, the sensitivity and volume of the data, complaint and legal limitation periods, accounting requirements, provider coordination needs and whether records are required to establish or defend legal claims. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it securely.

You may ask about the retention approach applying to a particular record.

9. Your UK GDPR rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to be informed, access your information, correct inaccurate information, request erasure, restrict processing, object to processing, receive portable data and withdraw consent. You may also have rights concerning solely automated decisions; we do not currently make treatment or finance decisions by solely automated means.

To exercise a right, email support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity. Rights are not absolute, and we will explain if an exemption applies.

10. Cookies and analytics

This website uses essential browser storage to remember your cookie preference and support effective operation. We do not currently load optional analytics or advertising cookies. If optional analytics are introduced, they will not be loaded until the required consent has been obtained, and this policy will be updated. You can reopen Cookie settings from the footer.

11. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will appear here with a revised date. Questions about this policy or our use of personal information should be sent to support@akdenizconsultancy.co.uk.
