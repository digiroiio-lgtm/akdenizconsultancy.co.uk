Terms & Conditions

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd provides healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support for UK patients considering dental treatment overseas. All clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning and dental treatment are carried out solely by independent overseas dental clinics and clinicians.

Our role

Akdeniz Dental Consultancy UK Ltd provides healthcare consultancy, patient coordination and administrative support only. We do not provide clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning or dental treatment.

Independent providers

All clinical care is provided solely by independent overseas clinics and qualified clinicians. The treating provider is responsible for clinical advice, consent, treatment, outcomes and clinical aftercare.

Information and decisions

Information we provide is general coordination information and is not medical advice. Patients must discuss clinical suitability, risks and alternatives directly with the treating clinician before proceeding.

Finance

Any finance is subject to eligibility, status and the finance provider's terms. Approval is not guaranteed.
